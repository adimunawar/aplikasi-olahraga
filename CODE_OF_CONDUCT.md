# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in Workout Coach to be constructive, respectful, and focused on improving the project. This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Explain technical disagreement with evidence and alternatives.
- Give actionable feedback about code, behavior, tests, and documentation.
- Welcome different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Respect privacy and do not publish personal data, credentials, or private communications.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Deliberate disruption, trolling, or repeated bad-faith participation.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sharing credentials, private user records, or vulnerability details publicly.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Maintainer responsibility

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior that violates this policy.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainer privately through GitHub. Do not use a public issue for sensitive personal information.

For security vulnerabilities, follow SECURITY.md instead.

## Scope

This policy applies to project spaces and public interactions when someone is representing or participating in the Workout Coach project.
